Welcome to Kizuna

A few steps and Kizuna is ready: it fetches new episodes and movies, adds subtitles in your language and puts them in Jellyfin. Each step checks itself; come back here any time from Settings.

Apps Kizuna uses

Kizuna uses FFmpeg to read and check videos and MKVToolNix to add the subtitle track. Install buttons open Windows' own installer (winget) in a separate window; follow it there, then press Check again.

    winget is missing: install "App Installer" from the Microsoft Store, or install the apps from their websites.

      Stremio

        Kizuna finds episodes and subtitles through the add-ons installed in your Stremio account. In the Stremio app or on Stremio Web, open Add-ons and install at least one add-on that provides streams you are allowed to use, and a subtitle add-on such as OpenSubtitles. Then sign in here.

        Your password goes only to Stremio's own sign-in. Kizuna keeps the session key, encrypted for your Windows user; the password is never stored.

        Open Stremio Web
